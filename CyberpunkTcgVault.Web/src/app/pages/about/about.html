ABOUT // CHOOM VAULT

A home for the collection.

Choom Vault is an independent, fan-made collector companion for Cyberpunk TCG, built around discovering, organising and living with a physical card collection.

01

What Choom Vault is

Built around the cards, not around a dashboard.

Choom Vault begins with the shared Vault Archive: a place to browse and discover cards through a focused collector-first catalogue.

Choom Vault connects the shared Vault Archive to a private collector workspace where signed-in collectors can manage exact card printings, wanted cards and sealed products. The product is designed around the physical collection itself — what you own, what you are still chasing and what you choose to keep sealed.

02

Why it exists

Why Choom Vault exists.

Choom Vault was built independently out of a genuine love for Cyberpunk, physical card collecting and the worlds that stay with you long after you leave them. It comes from an appreciation for the creative lineage behind that world — from Mike Pondsmith's tabletop Cyberpunk vision, through CD PROJEKT RED's interpretation and expansion of Night City, to WeirdCo and the people bringing Cyberpunk into a physical trading-card experience.

The collectors, players, artists, designers, developers and fans around Cyberpunk are part of the reason this project exists at all.

Choom Vault is an independent fan-made collector companion. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by those creators, companies or rights holders. It was built with respect for their work and for the people collecting and playing it.

CURRENT DEMONSTRATION

ORIGINAL MATERIAL. APPROVED ROUTES ONLY.

The current public demonstration uses Choom Vault prototype material created for this project. The Collector MVP already demonstrates the collector experience without depending on scraped or unauthorised official catalogue data.

Any future connection to official Cyberpunk TCG catalogue data or artwork will only be pursued through an approved data or API route, and within the boundaries set by the relevant rights holders and data provider.

Public registration is currently disabled while Choom Vault remains a limited hosted demonstration.

If Choom Vault remains a carefully made collector experience that people enjoy exploring, building it has already been worthwhile.

If an approved route to official Cyberpunk TCG catalogue data becomes available in future, that could allow the existing collector experience to go deeper. Any roadmap involving official content would only be defined once the permitted data, artwork and integration boundaries were understood.
