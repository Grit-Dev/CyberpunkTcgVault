ABOUT // CHOOM VAULT

A home for the collection.

Choom Vault is an independent, fan-made collector companion for Cyberpunk TCG, built around discovering, organising and living with a physical card collection.

01

What Choom Vault is

Built around the cards, not around a dashboard.

Choom Vault begins with the shared Vault Archive: a place to browse and discover cards through a focused collector-first catalogue.

Choom Vault connects the shared Vault Archive to a private collector workspace where signed-in collectors can manage exact card printings, wanted cards and sealed products. The product is designed around the physical collection itself — what you own, what you are still chasing and what you choose to keep sealed.

Those personal collector tools are part of the product direction and are introduced only when the underlying functionality is real.

02

Built for collectors

A collection should feel like more than rows in a database.

The aim is not simply to record that a card exists. Choom Vault is being shaped around the details collectors actually care about: the version they own, how many copies they have, what they are chasing and how the collection changes over time.

Know what you own.

Know where it is.

Keep the history of how your collection changes.

Product direction

Own. Trace. Express.

Three ideas guide where Choom Vault grows next without turning the project into a list of disconnected features.

01 / OWN

Own

Build and manage the collection itself: cards, exact printings, quantities, wanted cards and sealed products.

02 / TRACE

Trace

Explore deeper collector tools such as condition, physical storage, collection history, movement and verification.

Future direction — not all of these capabilities are currently live.
03 / EXPRESS

Express

Let a collection become personal through the future Safehouse: pinned cards, current chases, selected sealed items, milestones, music and collection history.

Future direction.
03

What's next

Deeper tools, introduced when they earn their place.

Future exploration includes faster card capture and import, set completion, physical collection verification, collection history, deck tools and the personal Safehouse experience.

There are no public release dates attached to those ideas. The priority is to make each part useful, coherent and connected to the collection itself.
