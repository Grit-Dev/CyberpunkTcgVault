About this notice

This notice explains how Choom Vault handles account information, private collector records and the services used to operate the application.

01 / ACCOUNT & ACCESS

Account & access information.

Choom Vault stores a username and email address for personal accounts so the account can be identified and private collector features can be provided.

Passwords are not stored as readable text. They are protected using the password-hashing provided by the authentication system.

Public registration is currently disabled. Demo Vault access is provided as a restricted evaluation environment and should not be used to store personal or sensitive information.

02 / COLLECTOR INFORMATION

Private collector information.

Private collector records can include the exact card printing you own or want, quantities, condition, notes and collector preferences recorded against Collection items; wanted quantities and Wishlist information; and sealed-product information such as product details, quantity, artwork URL, storage location and notes.

A storage location is text you choose to enter, such as “office cabinet” or “top shelf.”

Choom Vault does not use that field to track your device location or GPS position.

Storage location example Office cabinet · Top shelf

This is collector-entered private text. It describes where you choose to keep an item; it is not device-location tracking.

03 / COOKIES & BROWSER STORAGE

Authentication cookies & browser storage.

When you sign in, Choom Vault uses essential authentication and security cookies to maintain your session and protect account actions against unauthorised requests.

These cookies are used for the operation and security of the signed-in service.

The browser also uses short-lived session storage to preserve the current public Vault Archive view when you inspect a card and return. That temporary state contains Archive filters and page context, not authentication credentials or private collector records.

Choom Vault does not currently use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies.

04 / VISIBILITY

Visibility.

Your Collection, Wishlist and Sealed Product records are private account data and are not made public by default.

This includes collector notes and user-entered storage-location information.

The public Vault Archive and public Card Detail information are separate from your private collector records.

05 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

External services & hosting.

If you choose to save an external Artwork URL for a sealed product, your browser may contact the image host you supplied when that artwork is displayed.

Choom Vault relies on external infrastructure services to host and operate the application. Information about the services used by the live deployment will be kept current in this notice.

06 / YOUR CONTROLS

Your controls & account deletion.

Collection, Wishlist and Sealed Product records can be changed or removed using the controls available to your account.

Eligible normal accounts can also use Delete Account from the Account page.

The current application removes the account and its associated Collection, Wishlist and Sealed Product records from the active Choom Vault database and signs the session out.

Demo accounts cannot use this deletion flow.

07 / RETENTION & DATA RIGHTS

Retention & data rights.

Account and private collector information remains part of the active Choom Vault service until it is changed, removed or the account is deleted, subject to any retention that may be required for operating and securing the service.

Depending on the circumstances, data-protection law may give you rights relating to your personal information, including access, correction, deletion or restriction of its use.

For questions or requests relating to information held by Choom Vault, contact hello.choomvault@outlook.com.

If you have a concern about how Choom Vault handles your personal information, please contact Choom Vault first so the issue can be reviewed. You also have the right to raise a data-protection complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
