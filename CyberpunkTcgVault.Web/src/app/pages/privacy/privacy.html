About this notice

Choom Vault is being developed in stages. This notice distinguishes current behaviour from privacy principles described for features that are not yet publicly available.

01 / INFORMATION

Account and access information.

The public Vault Archive can be browsed without signing in to a Choom Vault account.

When account features are used, Choom Vault stores the account information needed to recognise you and provide access to your personal Vault.

Passwords are not stored as readable text. They are protected using established password-hashing techniques.

When you sign in, Choom Vault uses secure authentication to recognise your account and protect access to your private collection data.

Public account features are still being prepared for release. This notice will be updated when the information collected or the way it is used changes.

02 / COLLECTION DATA

Your collection records are linked to your account.

Owned cards, wishlist items and sealed-product records are linked to the signed-in account. Choom Vault protects those personal records so another collector cannot read or change them through their account.

The shared card catalogue is separate from your personal Vault. A card can be publicly discoverable without revealing whether you own it, want it or have recorded personal information about it.

Deeper collector information such as condition, physical storage, movement history and collection auditing forms part of the future product direction where it is not already available.

Exact physical storage information is treated as private information. It must never become publicly visible simply because another part of a collection is shared.

03 / VISIBILITY

Private unless you choose otherwise.

Personal collection information is private by default. Sharing should always be a deliberate choice.

If public Safehouse functionality is introduced later, you will choose what is presented there. Exact physical storage locations, private notes and sensitive collection history must never become public automatically.

Visibility controls will be introduced only when the corresponding sharing features actually exist.

04 / THIRD PARTIES

External services should be clear when they are used.

Choom Vault will clearly identify external services that process user information when those services are genuinely part of the public product.

This notice will be updated as external services are introduced, including an explanation of why they are used and how they relate to information handled by Choom Vault.

Unfinished or planned integrations are not presented here as active parts of the service.

05 / YOUR CONTROLS

Your data should remain manageable.

Collection, wishlist and sealed-product records can be changed or removed through the functionality available to the account.

A complete self-service account deletion flow is not yet live. It is a requirement before Choom Vault treats public user accounts as a complete product experience.

As additional sharing controls are introduced, they should remain understandable and private by default.

06 / CONTACT

Questions and data requests.

For privacy questions, data-related requests or rights concerns, visit the Contact / Rights page.

You can also contact Choom Vault directly at hello.choomvault@outlook.com.
